Restaurant Prediction App

An end-to-end ML app that forecasts next-day restaurant footfall from historical daily data, to support staffing and inventory planning. It uses 17 engineered features (day of week, seasonality, holidays, promotions, weather, lag and rolling-average demand) and a Gradient Boosting model with a time-based validation split. It reached about 6% average error on simulated data, roughly 60% lower than a "same day last week" baseline.
